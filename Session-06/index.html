Welcome CARATs!

Welcome to the official homepage for Jeon Wonwoo fans! This platform brings together CARATs from around the world to celebrate and support SEVENTEEN's main rapper and sub-vocalist, Wonwoo.

What We Do

Our community actively coordinates support projects to boost Wonwoo and SEVENTEEN's activities globally:

  • Organizing Support Projects: Birthday cup-sleeve events, subway billboard advertisements, and charitable donations.
  • Album & Streaming Parties: Coordinating digital streaming goals and group album purchasing.
  • Fan Gatherings: Hosting concert watch parties and offline CARAT meetups in local areas.
