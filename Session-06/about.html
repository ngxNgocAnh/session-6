About Jeon Wonwoo

Jeon Won-woo (전원우) was born on July 17, 1996, in Changwon, South Korea. He debuted on May 26, 2015, as a lead rapper and sub-vocalist of the renowned K-pop boy group SEVENTEEN under PLEDIS Entertainment.

Recognized for his deep vocal timbre, charismatic stage presence, iconic glasses style, and warm heart, Wonwoo is an active member of SEVENTEEN's Hip Hop Team and the special unit JXW (Jeonghan x Wonwoo).
